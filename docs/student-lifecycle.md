# Student lifecycle

## Admission

Applicants use `/dar-quraan-admission`. The employee-style application stores identity, contact and emergency details, education/employment, Quran history, goals, documents, and availability. Administration moves it through Pending, In Process, Provisionally Approved, and Final Approved. Rejection requires a reason. Final approval creates the Student and active Student Assignment. Email and SMS are optional; SMS requires Frappe SMS Settings.

## Program and weekly teaching

Programs define track, goal, riwayah/method, Jazariyyah requirements, and ordered milestones. Every active student should have a weekly individual slot of at least 30 minutes. Text Progress records memorization and explanation of Al-Jazariyyah or another approved text.

## Formal examinations

Use Exam for five-juz, 15-juz scientific committee, final 30-juz, final 10-juz, retest, and special exams. Committee exams require three unique members and one chair. Final formats enforce the specified question counts, 90 marks for memorization/performance/waqf and ibtida, 10 marks for Tajweed theory, and an 80% pass threshold. Error Rules control per-occurrence deductions.

## Ijazah

An Ijazah can be issued only from a completed passing final exam scoring at least 80%. It records the riwayah, reading method, granting sheikh, committee chair, connected sanad, certificate number/file, and revocation history.

## Discipline

Discipline Rules hold the institute policy, severity, default action, and guardian-notification requirement. Incidents link the rule to the student's assignment and require notification and resolution evidence when applicable.
